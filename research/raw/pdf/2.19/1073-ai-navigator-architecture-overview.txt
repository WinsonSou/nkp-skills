AI NAVIGATOR
AI Navigator provides real-time interactive responses to Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP)-related
queries using NKP and Kubernetes® documentation.
AI Navigator is an AI-powered application that delivers instant answers to queries ranging from basic
instructions to advanced NKP functionalities. Nutanix trains AI Navigator on NKP product documentation,
the Nutanix Support Knowledge Base, and upstream Kubernetes® documentation.
To use AI Navigator, ensure that the following requirements are met:
•
You have a Pro or Ultimate license for DKP version 2.6.0 or later, or NKP version 2.12 or later.
•
You accepted the user agreement.
•
Your environment has internet access.
Warning:  Nutanix does not support AI Navigator in air-gapped environments to maintain security for public
sector, defense, and network-restricted deployments.
AI Navigator Architecture Overview
AI Navigator integrates with Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP) clusters and external services to provide
secure and authorized access to AI-driven insights.
The AI Navigator architecture consists of core components that work together across different deployment
models to deliver AI-driven insights and cluster-specific information.
Core Components
•
AI Navigator app:
The AI Navigator app is the primary user-facing interface that provides access to AI-powered features
and coordinates interactions between backend services.
•
Retrieval-augmented generation:
The retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) engine sources relevant documents, enriches queries with
contextual information, and interfaces with large language models (LLMs) to generate responses.
•
NKP MCP Server:
Since NKP 2.19, the NKP MCP Server deploys automatically alongside AI Navigator. It injects cluster-
specific data into user queries and enriches the context for the RAG component to produce accurate
and relevant responses based on the target cluster's characteristics.
