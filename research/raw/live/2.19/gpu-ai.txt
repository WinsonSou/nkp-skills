== NFD ==
CAREN addon clusterConfig.addons.nfd {"strategy":"HelmAddon"}; namespace node-feature-discovery; DaemonSet node-feature-discovery-worker (tolerates all), Deployments -master, -gc; chart 0.19.0.
Lab nodes have label feature.node.kubernetes.io/pci-0300_1234.present (virtual VGA), no 10de labels (no NVIDIA GPU).
Batch 4 fix pass (2026-11-03): kubectl -n node-feature-discovery get configmap node-feature-discovery-worker-conf -o jsonpath='{.data}'
  -> nfd-worker.conf: sources.pci.deviceLabelFields: [class, vendor]. So NFD writes pci-<class>_<vendor>.present only; never pci-10de.present.
  NVIDIA: pci-0302_10de.present (3D controller) or pci-0300_10de.present (VGA) (pdf p.621 label fix uses pci-0302_10de).
